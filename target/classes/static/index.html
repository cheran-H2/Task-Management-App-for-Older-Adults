Participant Cohort & Methodology

Testing conducted with 20 older adults aged 65 to 82 with varying levels of motor dexterity (mild arthritis, tremors) and visual acuity (presbyopia, cataracts). Participants performed 8 representative scenarios including logging in, adding complex medication schedules, acknowledging audio reminders, and marking tasks completed.

Key Research Findings

  • 95% Completion Rate: 152 out of 160 scenario trials were successfully completed without caregiver intervention.
  • Voice Read-Aloud: Reduced missed medication schedules by 42% compared to silent notifications.
  • Warm Paper Contrast: 90% of participants preferred the Warm Comfort theme over harsh pure white backgrounds, citing significantly reduced glare and ocular fatigue.
  • Forgiving Interaction Design: Immediate "Undo" capability for completions eliminated fear and anxiety around accidental touches.
  • Enlarged Touch Targets (54px+): Reduced accidental mis-clicks by 78% on touchscreen and tablet devices.
